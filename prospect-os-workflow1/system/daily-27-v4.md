## Identity, historical and contact hard gates before any numerical ranking

G0 independent structured directory/registry/association/raw-original-ID discovery; search engines may VERIFY named entities but never directly populate discovery.
G1 actual official company identity and business: validate normalized domain, redirects, brand, legal/trading name, service/market from official + independent evidence; known mismatched site = REJECT_DOMAIN_MISMATCH, ambiguity = HOLD_IDENTITY.
G2 core independent/non-chain, 2–20 staff preferred, at most 50; 50+ or unclear = HOLD_SIZE until verified.
G3 cross-source history: old 103/112, central history, FB presented, last delivered batches, opt-outs and customer cases. Match parent/child and brand, domain, WA number, TG/FB identities. Incomplete scope = HISTORY_INCOMPLETE; known duplicate keeps original ID and is not new.
G4 dated relevant 30-day activity plus real business buyer role/problem; an evergreen homepage or a WhatsApp number alone does not prove recent activity. Unknown = HOLD_ACTIVITY, not fabricated PASS.
G5 business contact URL, human recipient ownership, actual marketing permission and policy acceptance are independent. Facebook company Page and publicly indexed personal profile are not tested personal Messenger; Telegram group/channel/bot not a direct person; WA published number is not opt-in or a recipient identity.
G6 AI claims require ACTUAL platform/model, nonbranded buyer question, exact answer, timestamp, context/language/location, observed brands, citations, original screenshot/session and SHA256. Never turn website text/search result into a fake model answer. An API result must be labeled API, not ChatGPT website.

Hard reject/hold beats score. No replicated 93.3 template grades.

## Evidence-grounded A selection score (0–100 only after hard checks)

0–30 credible buyer value and specialist high-ticket GEO relevance; 0–25 dated business/acquisition trigger; 0–20 verified decision access/contactability evidence; 0–15 bounded buyer-question and potential AI discovery opportunity; 0–10 implementability/language/compliance fit. Source and confidence per dimension; missing evidence = UNKNOWN and not full marks. Score is a work priority, never a sale or reply probability, and never grants contact permission.

## Independent stage dimensions

RAW_DISCOVERED → COMPANY_MATCHED → WORK_RESEARCH_READY or WORK_RESEARCH_READY_PROVISIONAL is a handoff path only, not an ICP_PASS proof. Preserve 2026-11-01 rule: can hand off identity-matched candidate with HOLD_ACTIVITY/HOLD_RECIPIENT/HISTORY_INCOMPLETE to Work so it can finish research; must not call it fully ICP qualified.

Separate evidence fields SITE_VERIFIED, BUYER_QUERY_PROPOSED, AI_TEST_PENDING, AI_SINGLE_TEST, AI_BASELINE_MULTI, ACTION_HYPOTHESIS, RETEST_OBSERVED. Missing true AI test => NOT_TESTED, not “AI did not recommend them.”

Separate company/route/recipient/permission/asset statuses. B may have TEXT_DRAFT_READY without image, and TEXT_CONTACT_REVIEW_READY only after verified permitted channel/recipient + human check; this text-only label is NOT image-first SEND_READY. ASSET_READY requires archived genuine original source capture and company-matched image/copy/manifest/link/hash; SEND_READY retains old strict ICP, history, original raw asset, verified human contact, consent/policy and human review gates. Neither status means SENT.

USER_CONFIRMED_SENT, REPLIED, SAMPLE_ACCEPTED, SAMPLE_DELIVERED, QUOTE_SENT, PAID and OPT_OUT are independently documented events; unknown events are UNKNOWN, not automatically zero.

## How to run A, B and R

A up to five strongest core prospects: actual buyer + service facts, then two natural unbranded buyer questions repeated twice per company when authorized AI platform is accessible. Record every response, citations, screenshot/URL/hash. If no accessible model/approved resources, mark AI_TEST_PENDING and hand off to Work; never invent outputs. When customer accepts sample, do six distinct purchasing scenarios and prepare a concrete proposal/retest plan.

B up to nineteen: company facts, one grounded buyer question, short natural MultiHub first contact (normally 25–45 English words/local equivalent), one easy-answer CTA. NO pre-required AI testing or PNG. Draft only; require legal/policy/opt-in before any contact. No attachment claim without attachment. B upgrades to paid-quality sample research upon explicit interest.

R three regulated WA study seats: RESEARCH_ONLY by default, document operating and platform legitimacy, no product ads or unsolicited restricted-product messaging.
