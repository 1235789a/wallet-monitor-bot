# Precision first-contact evidence packs

These explicit user instructions, recorded 2026-10-24, extend normal drafting.

## Automatic allocation memory

After delivering any list, persist its normalized company/domain/parent/chat
identities as provided. A next-batch request or topic change must not reissue
the previous list. Reconcile the live tracker before another allocation.
Provided, contacted, replied and opted-out are separate facts. Refinement uses
the original ID and does not increase unique-company totals.

Check the WA/TG tracker, Facebook presented registry and central History links
in `.agents/memory/prospecting-preferences.md`. Preserve source-specific status
conflicts; imported bulk `REPLIED` labels are not verified response events.
Report the exact sources and ranges checked, not an unsupported global dedupe.

## 精做 trigger

When the user requests 精做, create the evidence images and matching first copy
without requesting another confirmation to research or make the images. Freeze
the user-requested refinement cohort separately from the formal 30-company
Sample Lock. Do not silently replace a weak finding with an invented one.

For every company, understand and record:

- actual product/service and ideal paying buyer;
- purchase journey, decision-maker role, comparison criteria and commercial
  terms (including integration, logistics, scope or procurement as relevant);
- at least two primary-source product/commercial/proof signals;
- a concrete buyer question and one verified issue in the inspected material;
- the likely business consequence, explicitly an inference, and a specific
  improvement that the sender can honestly offer;
- current route ownership/purpose evidence and actual outreach status.

Do not reduce the research to a logo, sector, location or generic need for leads.
Share sector context where useful, but each company's finding must be its own.

## Evidence-image contract

Deliver a mobile-legible PNG with an honest headline, the real captured page or
clearly labelled source excerpt, a short factual annotation, the buyer question,
one scoped implication/proposal, source URL and capture date. Preserve the raw
screenshot/HTML, text, SHA-256 and capture conditions. Cosmetic callouts must not
alter page content. Do not use generative images to simulate factual evidence.

The image is meant to expose a verifiable issue quickly and earn a relevant
conversation. It must not manufacture fear, missing features, losses or urgency.
If the page is already clear, do not invent a problem merely to fill the quota.

Use explicit evidence kinds:

- `onsite_observation`: a fact about the inspected page/viewport; no inference
  about the whole site, backend or search visibility.
- `search_sample`: an actually executed query; record platform, time, language,
  location when available, result count and limited scope. Absence from a
  bounded sample is not an overall ranking or visibility diagnosis.
- `ai_answer_test`: an actual platform session with prompt and answer evidence.
  Search snippets or ordinary web results are not an AI-answer test.

Keep observed facts, interpretation and proposal visually distinct. A proposal
is not evidence that a feature is absent. No unsupported competitor comparison,
traffic/revenue estimate or claim that the company loses customers.

## Image and message together

The first-contact pack must include the finished image and a matching concise
caption, the attachment filename, the exact public business route, company
research card and source manifest. Use the recipient's working language and no
sender-name placeholder. One real detail, one clear point, one low-friction CTA.
Do not ask whether to prepare evidence that the user already requested.

For an unconfirmed marketing recipient, the caption may reference a scoped,
already observed onsite fact and ask whether this is the appropriate business
recipient. It may not claim a tested buyer-search/AI gap, jump to a meeting or
promise an outcome. Support-only, bot-only, order and booking-only routes remain
ineligible. Full search/competitive claims retain their original evidence gates.

`PRECISION_EVIDENCE` is an attachment-preparation workflow, not a handwritten
PRELOCK_PASS or a replacement for the formal quotas. Preserve actual gate
outcomes, untested human replies and permission boundaries.

## Delivery and sending

Save finished PNGs, raw evidence, the source-backed report and first-copy table
to Google Drive. Link refinements to original IDs. Keep received contact status
unchanged. A drafting skill prepares attachments and captions; an explicit user
request with a connected sender is required for the separate sending workflow.
Record sent only after success; unavailable sender connections must be stated.
